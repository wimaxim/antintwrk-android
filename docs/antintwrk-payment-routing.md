# Managed payment routing — 11 October 2026

Kindness is gifted points. Google Play Billing handles Kindness packages, the blue verification badge and ANTINTWRK project support. Project support grants Kindness from the immutable server order after a verified receipt; the history distinguishes project support from ordinary packages. Three proposed support products remain inactive until the actual Play catalog is configured. Prices come from Google ProductDetails, never a client-side USD conversion.

The native catalog accepts only verification, kindness and support, with supported product types. Kindness and support use one-time purchases. A failed refresh invalidates the previous checkout. The new client announces support_v1 so older clients retain their compatible catalog. Server receipts, actor/order bindings, replay protection, refund accounting and durable delivery before acknowledgement remain authoritative. Never grant from a launch result or place purchase tokens in WebView.

Charitable assistance preserves gifted Kindness after verified payment. Foundation and Super David open exact beneficiary paths with source=heroworld and a bounded signed referral. Gift policy is checked by the recipient and preserved in the payment snapshot, rather than accepted from an unsigned query flag. Historical payment snapshots retain their original policy.

Ukraine opens the public https://antintwrk.com/LANG/ukraine page in the external browser. A signed app_ref scopes donor authority to this campaign and expiry; it does not log the browser into the social account. Native navigation keeps this address public on either social origin and excludes it from embedded pages and public sharing. The bridge reconstructs the URL from the exact allowed public origin, localized path and referral; this also avoids proxy rewriting of an absolute public URL. The first-load submit handler works without any history navigation and is not repeatedly installed by pushState/replaceState.

Returning from the browser reads the balance without page reload or a repeated grant. Settings offers Kindness and project support in all nine supported languages. The native charitable view does not embed payment entry; completed payments are confirmed by the existing provider workers. A browser launch or return is never evidence of payment.

Validation:11 StoreCoordinator,2 NativeWebActions,28 WebFeed tests and appnav formatting passed;7 Billing transport tests also passed. Backend validation includes receipts, purchase/refund ledger, gift replay and authenticated external handoff on both social origins. Actual Play checkout is not yet accepted: merchant/products/Publisher/RTDN and a Play-installed license-test build are required before enabling sales. No AAB or APK has been uploaded to Play in this phase. No policy approval is inferred from the name “gift points”.
